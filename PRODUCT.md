# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite (multi-page, static) + three.js + GSAP (ScrollTrigger) + Lenis, all on GSAP's ticker. Chosen by Luca on 2026-10-28: "Video world like Emons", so three.js is used offline only, to render the world film and stills; the shipped page plays an MP4 and never runs WebGL.

## Users

- Businesses that import or export between Albania, the Balkans and the EU (shippers, buyers, small manufacturers) who need a forwarder for sea, air, road, customs and warehousing.
- A smaller lane of private senders: the Albanian diaspora sending parcels or goods to and from Italy, Germany and the UK.

## Product Purpose

A prototype website for UDHA, an invented Albanian freight forwarder, built to show what an Emons-style site (www.emons.de, studied 2026-10-28) looks like for an Albanian company: a scroll-stepped world film in the hero, a mega menu, freight inquiry and shipment tracking entry points. Success = Luca can show it as a convincing prototype; nothing in it is passed off as a real company.

## Positioning

Undecided: the company is invented. The prototype positions it as the forwarder that covers the whole Albanian corridor in one hand, from the port of Durrës and Rinas airport through Tirana to the Kosovo border at Morinë.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive to get a price (freight inquiry), track a shipment, or check which modes and routes are covered. Real geography may be used (Durrës port, Rinas airport, Tirana, Rruga e Kombit, Morinë crossing, ferries to Bari and Ancona).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Languages: Albanian (default), English, Italian. Italian and English copy should be checked by a native speaker before any real use.
- No back end: the inquiry form and tracking are demos and must say so.
- Not deployed; runs on localhost only until Luca says "deploy".

## Brand Commitments

Name UDHA ("the way" in Albanian), invented for this prototype (Luca, 2026-10-28: "Invented brand").

## Evidence on Hand

None. There are no real clients, fleet sizes, years, turnover, certifications, prices or transit times. Every number shown is a labelled example and listed in NEEDS_CONTENT.md. Factual reference content (euro pallet size, Incoterms 2020, customs documents) may be used.

## Product Principles

1. Invent nothing that reads as fact: example numbers are labelled as examples.
2. The world shows real Albanian geography, stylised.
3. Every way in (inquiry, tracking, phone, private parcels) is one click from every screen.
4. The film is the show, never the gate: skip, stills, keyboard and reduced motion all reach the content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the target; the hero's scroll stepping must work by keyboard, by buttons and with motion stopped.
